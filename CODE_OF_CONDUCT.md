# ShelterPact Community Rules

We welcome animal shelter staff, animal control professionals, rescue organizations, partners, and others interested in ShelterPact.

1. Be respectful, constructive, and professional.
2. Stay on topic and describe issues clearly.
3. Protect privacy and confidentiality. Never publish citizen/owner records, case details, contact information, credentials, or confidential agency materials.
4. Use anonymized sample data when asking for troubleshooting help.
5. Do not post spam, advertising, harassment, hateful content, or malicious links.
6. Do not post security vulnerabilities or exploit details publicly. Contact support@shelterpact.com privately.
7. Follow applicable laws and your organization's policies.

Moderators may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary.
