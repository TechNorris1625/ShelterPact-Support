# How to get support

## Public questions

Use GitHub Discussions for general how-to questions, troubleshooting, feedback, and product ideas. Search existing posts first. Give your discussion a clear title and describe what you expected, what happened, and the relevant ShelterPact screen or feature.

## Private or sensitive requests

Email **support@shelterpact.com** for confidential matters, security concerns, account-access issues, incidents involving personal data, or screenshots with sensitive information. Never place confidential agency or animal-owner data in a public discussion.

## Bug reports

If safe to share publicly, include:

1. Feature or page where the issue occurred.
2. Steps to reproduce using test data only.
3. Expected behavior and observed behavior.
4. Browser and device type.
5. Redacted screenshots, if helpful.

Do not share database exports, authentication tokens, logins, production records, or server details. If the bug involves confidential data, email private support instead.

## Scope

Community replies do not guarantee response times or constitute legal, veterinary, or regulatory advice. Organizational policies and applicable laws take precedence.
