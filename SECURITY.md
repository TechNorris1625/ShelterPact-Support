# Security reporting

**Do not disclose security vulnerabilities in public issues or discussions.**

To report a suspected vulnerability or data exposure, contact **support@shelterpact.com** with a subject such as `Security report — ShelterPact`. Provide a concise description and avoid sending unnecessary personal data. We will coordinate follow-up privately.

Do not attempt unauthorized access, broad scanning, or exploitation of ShelterPact systems or customer environments.
